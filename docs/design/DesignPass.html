Only your Mac will show

  • How the glass really looks over your own desktop picture. It changes with whatever is behind the window.
  • The drag picture is drawn by macOS. I can style the row it shows and remove the green plus, but the exact lift and tilt may be a little different.
  • Dropped into Finder or Claude, a recording still becomes a text file. There macOS shows the green plus, and that's correct, since it's a copy.
  • If Reduce transparency is on in your Mac's Accessibility settings, the sidebar stays solid even when Glass is picked.
