Mini window, new design

A mockup you can click. It is not the real app yet. The words below are made up and arrive by themselves, like in class. Nothing gets built until you say this is right.

Try it

  1. Click any line of text and fix a word. Press Enter to save, Esc to cancel. Watch what happens to new words while you type.
  2. Press Marker, Copy new, Pause, then Stop. After Stop you see the new "not recording" look.
  3. Press the × at the top left. The window goes away but the recording keeps going. Click the timer in the fake menu bar to bring it back.

What changes

  • No more empty strip on topThe window loses its hidden title bar, which was holding that space. The first row sits right at the top.
  • A close button× at the top left, where Mac windows have it. It only hides the window. Recording never stops because you closed it.
  • Not bare when idleIt says it is ready, which mic it will use, a real Start button, and your last recording with Copy all.
  • Buttons have namesA small word under each icon, so you don't have to hover to learn what they do.
  • Fix text right hereClick a line to type in it. It shows the last 3 lines instead of 2 so there is more to grab.
  • Editing while words keep comingThe line you are fixing stays still and is never changed under you. New words start a new line below it. If more than 2 new lines pile up, it just counts them. When you save, the view goes back to the newest words.
