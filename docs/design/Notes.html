Notes on lines

A mockup you can click. It is not the real app yet. For the things the mic can't hear, like "Mr. Yurt wrote the essay question on the board". The words are made up and keep arriving, like in class.

Try it

  1. Right-click a line (or press and hold on a phone) and pick Add note. Type something, press Enter.
  2. Press the Note button at the top, or ⇧⌘N. That puts a note on the line being said right now, for when you're in a hurry.
  3. Click a note to change it. Clear the text and press Enter to delete it. Every change has Undo at the bottom.
  4. Scroll down the page to see how notes look when you copy the transcript for Claude.

How it works

    
    
      
  • Where a note sitsRight under its line, a bit smaller, with a pencil in front and a warmer color, so it never looks like something that was said.
    • 
      
  • Three ways to add oneRight-click a line and pick Add note. The Note button in the toolbar. Or ⇧⌘N, which notes the line being said right now.
    • 
      
  • Typing while words arriveThe transcript stops scrolling while you type, and your line and note stay put. New lines keep getting added below.
    • 
      
  • Change or deleteClick a note to edit it. Empty it and press Enter to delete, or right-click the line and pick Delete note. Undo for a few seconds at the bottom.
    • 
      
  • One note per lineSimple on purpose. If you want to add more to a line that has a note, you just edit the note.
    • 
      
  • Counted at the topThe header says how many notes the class has, next to the markers.
